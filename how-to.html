Writing your tag

Every sibni tag ships blank. Here's how to fill it in, and what to think about before you do.

Before you start: anyone can read it

NFC tags aren't password-protected on read — that's the whole point, so a stranger can help without needing an app or account. It also means you shouldn't put anything on it you wouldn't want a stranger to see. Emergency contacts and key health facts are a good fit; banking details or passwords are not.

1

Get a free NFC-writing app

We recommend NFC Tools (free, iOS and Android) — it's simple and doesn't need an account. Any similar app that can write "Text" or "Contact" records will work too.

2

Decide what to include

It's entirely up to you, but most people include some mix of:

  • Your name
  • An emergency contact — name and phone number
  • Key health information: allergies, conditions, medication
  • Anywhere relevant: "please take me to [address / clinic name]"
3

Write it in the app

Open NFC Tools, go to Write, and add a record. A Contact record works well since most phones open it directly in the contacts app on tap. If you'd rather write plain text, a Text record works too — just keep it short and readable.

4

Hold the tag to your phone

Your phone's NFC antenna is usually near the top back of the device. Hold the tag against it and tap "Write" in the app. It takes about a second.

5

Test it

Lock your phone, then tap the tag with it — or better, ask a friend to tap it with theirs. Confirm the right information shows up before you rely on it.

Optional: locking the tag

NFC Tools has a "Lock tag" or "Write protect" option. Locking makes the tag permanently read-only — it can still be tapped and read by anyone, forever, but nobody (including you) can write to it again.

This cannot be undone

Locking is a one-way, physical change to the chip. There is no password, reset, or sibni support process that can reverse it. Before you lock a tag:

  • Re-read everything on it carefully — check spelling and phone numbers
  • Test it with a second phone
  • Be sure the information won't need updating soon

If you're at all unsure, leave the tag unlocked. You can always come back and lock it later, once you're confident it's right.
