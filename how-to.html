Set up your tag

Your sibni tag arrives with HELP. SCAN NFC on it and an NFC chip ready for you to write your own link or information.

Before you start: anyone can read it

NFC tags are designed to be read by someone with an NFC-capable phone. Don't put anything on the tag you wouldn't want a stranger to see. Emergency contacts and useful instructions are a good fit; passwords and sensitive financial information are not.

1

Get an NFC-writing app

We recommend NFC Tools or another reputable NFC-writing app available for your phone.

2

Choose what you want the tag to open

A URL is often the easiest option — for example, a personal emergency page, contact page or other information you want a helper to access.

3

Write it to the tag

In your NFC app, choose the option to write a record, add your URL or information, then start the write process.

4

Hold the tag to your phone

Place the NFC area of the tag against the NFC antenna on your phone and follow the app's instructions. The write normally takes only a moment.

5

Test it

Use a second phone if possible. Make sure the correct link or information appears before you rely on the tag.

Optional: lock the tag

Some NFC apps allow you to make the tag permanently read-only. If you use that feature, check everything first because a locked tag may not be writable again.

Only lock it when you're certain

Test the tag first and make sure you won't need to change the information later.
