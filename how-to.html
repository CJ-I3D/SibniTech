How it works

Your Sibni tag arrives ready to be programmed. Follow these steps and you'll have it working in a few minutes.

First, you need three things

  1. Your Sibni tag.
  2. A phone with NFC turned on.
  3. An NFC-writing app. We recommend NFC Tools.

You do not need to install an app on the phone of the person scanning your tag.

1

Decide what you want someone to see

The simplest option is a website link. For example, you could use a personal emergency-information page containing your name, emergency contacts, allergies, medical information or anything else you want a finder to know.

Important: don't put passwords, banking details or other information on the tag that you wouldn't want a stranger to access.

2

Install an NFC-writing app

Install NFC Tools or another reputable NFC-writing app from your phone's normal app store.

You only need the writing app while setting up or changing your tag. Someone scanning the finished tag normally does not need the app.

3

Open the app and choose “Write”

Open the NFC app and look for an option called Write, Write a tag, or similar. Do not choose the read/scan option - you're setting the tag up, not reading it.

4

Add your information

Choose Add a record (the wording can vary slightly between apps). If you're adding a website, choose URL / Website and paste or type the full web address.

Check the address carefully before continuing. A typo here means the tag will point to the wrong place.

5

Press “Write”

Once your record is added, press Write or Write to tag. Your phone should then tell you to bring an NFC tag close to the phone.

6

Touch the Sibni tag to your phone

Place the tag against the part of your phone where the NFC antenna is located. Keep the tag still for a couple of seconds.

If nothing happens, slowly move the tag around the back of the phone until the phone detects it. Different phones have their NFC antenna in different places.

7

Wait for the confirmation

Your app should confirm that the tag was written successfully. Do not move the tag away until the app says the write is complete.

8

Test the tag

Now test it before relying on it. Ideally, use a different phone to scan it. Hold the tag close to the phone and check that the correct link or information appears.

If the wrong thing appears, simply write the correct information to the tag again.

9

Put the tag where you need it

Attach it to your keys, lanyard, shoes, bag, pet accessory or other intended item. The tag is now ready to use.

Want to change the information later?

You can rewrite the tag whenever you need to. You control the data on it - Sibni does not store it, manage it or need access to it.

If you permanently lock the tag, however, you may not be able to change it afterwards. Only use the lock function once you're completely sure the information is final.

Make checking it a habit

It is worth randomly checking your Sibni tag from time to time to make sure it still scans and still shows the information you expect. This is especially useful if you have left the NFC edits unlocked.

Severe impacts can damage the internal NFC antenna even if the outside of the tag looks fine. With children, tags can also be swapped between bags, shoes or other items without anyone noticing.

A quick scan every so often can catch a problem before the tag is actually needed.
