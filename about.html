About Sibni

Sibni is simply the Maltese word for "find me".

We started Sibni around a simple idea: emergency information is only useful if someone can actually find it. An NFC tag gives a finder a simple way to access the information you've chosen to share, with no special app needed on their smartphone.

We make a range of small 3D-printed designs for keys, lanyards, shoes, bags, pets and everyday carry. The business-card version is especially thin, giving you a discreet option when you want something less bulky.

What goes on the tag is entirely up to you. You write the information directly to the tag from your own phone, and you can change it whenever you need to. Sibni doesn't see, store or control your NFC data.

What we believe

Simple beats clever

A tag that a compatible phone can read, with no app required for the finder, is more useful than a system that depends on special software.

You're in control

You choose what's written, when to update it, and whether to lock it. Your data stays with you.

No batteries

Sibni tags don't need batteries, charging or battery replacements. The NFC tag is powered by the phone that reads it.

Recycled plastic

Our 3D-printed tags are made using recycled plastic, keeping the product lightweight while making use of recycled material.

No MOQs

Need one tag? That's fine. Need a batch? That's fine too. We don't impose minimum order quantities.

Built to be worn

A tag only helps if it's actually with you or your belongings. We design around the ways people already carry their keys, shoes, bags and everyday items.
