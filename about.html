About sibni

Sibni is the Maltese word for "find me."

We started sibni around a simple problem: emergency information only helps if someone can actually find it, and most people don't have a good place to put it. A note in a wallet gets missed. A medical bracelet is one more thing to remember to buy. A phone lock screen message only works if the finder knows to look.

An NFC tag solves the "finding it" part — one tap, on almost any phone made in the last decade, no app required on the other end. So we built a small range of 3D-printed mounts — for a shoelace, a keyring, a carabiner, a lanyard — around the same tiny chip, so there's a way to carry one whatever you're already doing: running, hiking, at school, at a festival, or just day to day.

Each tag is printed in small batches. What goes on the chip is entirely up to you — we don't write it, store it, or ever see it. That's not a compliance decision, it's just how the product works: the writing happens directly between your phone and the tag, with nothing in between.

What we believe

Simple beats clever

A tag that anyone's phone can read, with no app, is more useful in an emergency than a system that depends on the finder having the right software.

You're in control

You choose what's written, when to update it, and whether to lock it. We don't have a dashboard into any of it — there isn't one.

Built to be worn

A tag only helps if it's actually on you. We design for the way people already carry their keys, shoes, and bags.
