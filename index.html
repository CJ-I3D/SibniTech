Sibni means
"find me."

A small NFC tag, 3D-printed to clip onto a shoe, a keyring, a bag or a lanyard. You decide what it says. Anyone who taps it can help.

Four ways to carry it

Same NTAG216 chip inside every tag. Pick whichever fits what you already carry.

We never see what you write

The tag is blank when it arrives. You write to it directly, phone to tag. Nothing passes through sibni's servers — because there are none involved in that step.

You write it

A free app writes straight to the chip. No sibni account, no upload, no cloud step.

You can lock it

Once you're happy with it, you can make the tag permanently read-only. We'll walk you through what that means before you do it.

You can rewrite it

If you don't lock it, write it again any time — new contact, new season, new sport.
