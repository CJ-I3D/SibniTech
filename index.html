Sibni means
"find me."

A small NFC tag, 3D-printed in a range of shapes for shoes, bags, keys, pets and more. Every tag says HELP. SCAN NFC so someone knows what to do.

Choose your tag

Different shapes and ways to carry it. Same simple idea: HELP. SCAN NFC.

Ready to set up when it arrives

Your tag isn't a blank product waiting for us to finish it. It arrives with HELP. SCAN NFC on it and an NFC chip ready for you to program. The How it works guide walks you through writing your own link or information.

You choose the content

Write your own link, contact information or other useful details directly to the NFC tag.

We don't see it

Your phone writes directly to the tag. Your NFC content doesn't pass through sibni.

You can update it

Unless you choose to lock the tag, you can rewrite the NFC content later.
