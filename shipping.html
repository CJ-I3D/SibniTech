Collect your tag in person at no charge. While you're there, we'll help you write your NFC data on the spot - free of charge, no need to figure out the app yourself beforehand if you'd rather not.

€7 flat rate

Courier delivery - Malta

A flat €7 covers courier delivery anywhere in Malta, regardless of order size. After you order, we'll contact you by phone or email to schedule a delivery slot that works for you.

€15 flat rate

Courier delivery - Gozo

A flat €15 covers courier delivery anywhere in Gozo. As with Malta deliveries, we'll contact you to schedule a convenient slot.

From €4.50

International delivery

Sent via MaltaPost. Pick your country at checkout and the price fills in automatically - it depends on which MaltaPost zone your country falls in (Europe is cheapest, at €4.50). We currently cover major destinations across Europe, North Africa, the Americas, the Gulf, and parts of Asia and Oceania. Don't see your country listed? Get in touch. MaltaPost may still cover it, we just haven't added it yet.

Why we ask for a phone number

Courier deliveries in Malta and Gozo are scheduled individually - someone on our end will call or message you to arrange a slot that suits you, rather than a courier turning up unannounced.
