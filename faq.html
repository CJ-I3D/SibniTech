Frequently asked

Does Sibni store or see my NFC data?

No. You write directly from your phone to the tag. Your NFC content is not uploaded to Sibni.

Is the tag ready to set up when it arrives?

Yes. The NFC tag is ready for you to write your own link or information using the how-to guide.

What can I put on it?

A URL is a simple choice, but you can use whatever NFC record your writing app supports. Think about what would genuinely help someone who finds you, your bag or your pet.

What phones can read it?

Most modern iPhones and Android phones with NFC can read an NFC tag. The reader normally does not need the same app you used to write it.

Can I change what's written later?

Yes, as long as the tag has not been permanently locked.

Should I check my tag from time to time?

Yes. We recommend randomly checking that the tag still scans and still shows the correct information, especially if it has not been permanently locked. Severe impacts can damage the internal NFC antenna, and with children in particular, tags can also be swapped between items.

Can I lock it?

Some NFC-writing apps offer permanent write protection. Only use it after testing the tag and confirming you won't need to update it.

Are the tags waterproof?

The 3D-printed shells are intended for everyday use and exposure, but they are not presented as certified waterproof products. Avoid prolonged submersion.

Do you provide a lanyard or keychain?

The tag itself can be used with your own lanyard or keychain. We do not include the lanyard or keychain with the tag. If you want us to supply a lanyard, that can be quoted separately.

Can I order a custom shape?

Yes. Choose the custom tag form and we'll make it to specification. Custom pricing may change depending on size, complexity and materials.

How do the discounts work?

Choose the Random colour combination option for 10% off. Orders containing 5 or more items receive a further 10% off. Carabiners are an optional €1 add-on and are always supplied in random colours.

Are the dimensions exact?

Listed dimensions are approximate and may have a small margin of error due to the 3D-printing process.

What can you do for custom tags?

We can produce custom shapes and colours and can print directly in full colour, including logos, graphics and text, subject to the requirements of the order. Custom pricing is P.O.R.
