Frequently asked

Does sibni store or see my NFC data?

No. You write directly from your phone to the tag. Your NFC content is not uploaded to sibni.

Is the tag blank when it arrives?

No. The physical tag arrives with HELP. SCAN NFC on it. The NFC chip is ready for you to write your own link or information using the how-to guide.

What can I put on it?

A URL is a simple choice, but you can use whatever NFC record your writing app supports. Think about what would genuinely help someone who finds you, your bag or your pet.

What phones can read it?

Most modern iPhones and Android phones with NFC can read an NFC tag. The reader normally does not need the same app you used to write it.

Can I change what's written later?

Yes, as long as the tag has not been permanently locked.

Can I lock it?

Some NFC-writing apps offer permanent write protection. Only use it after testing the tag and confirming you won't need to update it.

Are the tags waterproof?

The 3D-printed shells are intended for everyday use and exposure, but they are not presented as certified waterproof products. Avoid prolonged submersion.

Can I order a custom shape?

Yes. Choose the Custom Tag and we'll make it to specification. Custom pricing may change depending on size, complexity and materials.
